The public HQ MP3 preview was downloaded from the source page:
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/850/850275_6775466-hq.mp3

Adaptation: four 170 ms mono contacts, trimmed from the recording, DC offset
removed, peak-normalized and given short fades. Runtime playback alternates
the samples with small pitch/volume variations, triggered by grounded steps.
No looping of the original walk recording.

Rebuild: run tools/decode_footsteps.gd with Godot, then tools/prepare_footsteps.py.
