Original procedural foley, synthesized for Tales of a Hundred Spirits.
Generator: tools/synthesize_combat_audio.py (deterministic NumPy synthesis).
No external recordings, voices or music used.

silk_charge: gathering, tensioned fibers; 700 ms.
silk_shot: short fiber snap and airy trailing swish; 230 ms.
silk_burst: one layered fan-shaped release for the eight-way volley; 480 ms.
umbrella_swing: dry cloth/wood movement through air; 190 ms.
umbrella_hit_crisp_v2: bright dry crack, 0.25 ms attack, reduced low/mid weight;
110 ms. Current impact sound. The superseded umbrella_hit.wav was removed.

Runtime levels and playback live in scripts/combat_sounds.gd.
Only confirmed hits play umbrella_hit; misses play umbrella_swing alone.
Hit-stop does not interrupt the impact sound. Pausing stops combat audio.
