Original procedural foley, synthesized for Tales of a Hundred Spirits.
Generator: tools/synthesize_combat_audio.py (deterministic NumPy synthesis).
No external recordings, voices or music used.

silk_charge: gathering, tensioned fibers; 700 ms.
silk_shot: short fiber snap and airy trailing swish; 230 ms.
silk_burst: one layered fan-shaped release for the eight-way volley; 480 ms.
umbrella_swing: dry cloth/wood movement through air; 190 ms.
umbrella_hit_crisp_v2: bright dry crack, 0.25 ms attack, reduced low/mid weight;
110 ms. Current impact sound. The old umbrella_hit.wav is retained for reference.

Second generator: tools/synthesize_impact_audio.py (same method, added 2026-11-25).
umbrella_hit_heavy_v3: current impact sound. Crisp crack kept on top, with a
second crunch transient, a woody knock, a low thump and a bright rising
"shing" tail; heavily saturated; 340 ms.
umbrella_hit_crisp_v2 is retained for reference.
umbrella_block: strike stopped by the Peach Spirit's guard; hard clack with a
bright ringing rebound and no low end; 340 ms.
boss_swing: heavy branch sweep; 320 ms.
boss_lunge: rising rush for thrusts, dashes and dives; 300 ms.
boss_slam: landing thud after a dive or leap; 420 ms.
petal_gust: airy swell when the blossom storm is released; 1.1 s.

Runtime levels and playback live in scripts/combat_sounds.gd.
Only confirmed hits play umbrella_hit; misses play umbrella_swing alone.
Hit-stop does not interrupt the impact sound. Pausing stops combat audio.
