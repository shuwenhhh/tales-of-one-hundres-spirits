Transparent silhouettes extracted from the generated sheet by connected components
at alpha >= 0.92 to exclude generated background glow. Shared runtime scale 0.32.
These are six key poses with procedural movement, not a full frame-by-frame animation
or a Skeleton2D rig. Silk is drawn independently as three interwoven curves with
